? WHAT IS A KNOWLEDGE GRAPH
PRIMER [ CLOSE ]

A knowledge graph stores facts as a network: nodes are entities (an artist, a city, an instrument) and edges are the relationships between them ("recorded in", "plays", "evolved from").

It is built by ingesting sources — archives, logs, datasets — and extracting entities and relations from each. As more sources arrive, separate islands of knowledge link up.

Here we reconstruct 20th-century American music. Watch the Delta Blues, Soul and Rock clusters appear as isolated worlds — then watch one real session-musician collective in Muscle Shoals bridge them into a single navigable web.
