WHAT IS A KNOWLEDGE GRAPH?

A knowledge graph stores facts as nodes (entities: people, papers, institutions, techniques) connected by edges (relationships: wrote, cites, introduced, funded).

SUBSTRATE.idx is a mock archival indexer. It "crawls" sources one at a time. Each source is parsed into entities and relations, which the plotter inks onto the sheet live. Every line in the build log is a real handle to a node or edge on the graph — hover a line to light it up, hover a node to flash its line.

The story: two AI research traditions grow up separately (connectionist vs. statistical NLP) until "Attention Is All You Need" (2017) cites both — bridging the cut and fusing the graph into one connected component.
